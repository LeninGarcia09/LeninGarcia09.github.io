# Phase 4: Value Realization + Content + Networking (Weeks 17–21)

> **Objective:** Convert technical artifacts into a **business value narrative** and **public visibility**. A senior AI Solution Architect does not just build: they connect the solution to KPIs and communicate it. Here you produce a value realization framework, an applied case with a KPI tree, and two technical publications.

## 🎯 Expected Outcomes

By completing this phase:
- **Value realization framework** v1 (how the ROI of an AI solution is measured)
- One **applied case** with a **KPI tree** connecting the solution to business outcomes
- **2 technical publications** (blog, long-form LinkedIn, or repo write-up) published
- Network activated with targeted outreach
- Ready for **Gate CP4 (Week 21)**

---

## Week 17 — Value realization framework v1

**Objective:** define how you measure the value of an AI solution end to end.

| Resource | Language | Type |
|---------|--------|------|
| [Microsoft: AI value realization / business case](https://learn.microsoft.com/es-es/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/scenarios/ai/strategy) | 🇪🇸 | Guide |
| [Cloud Adoption Framework — Business outcomes](https://learn.microsoft.com/es-es/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/strategy/business-outcomes/) | 🇪🇸 | Framework |

**Deliverable:** document `value-framework-v1` — how you move from a technical capability (RAG, agent) to business metrics (savings, revenue, risk avoided, cycle time).

## Week 18 — Applied case #1 + KPI tree

**Objective:** apply the framework to your own Phase 2/3 system.

| Resource | Language | Type |
|---------|--------|------|
| [KPI trees / driver trees](https://learn.microsoft.com/es-es/power-bi/create-reports/service-goals-introduction) | 🇪🇸 | Concept |

**Deliverable:** applied case connecting your AI solution to a **KPI tree** (business KPI → drivers → technical metric) and a defensible impact estimate.

## Week 19 — Technical publication #1

**Objective:** demonstrate expertise in public.

| Resource | Language | Type |
|---------|--------|------|
| [Guide: writing a good technical post](https://developers.google.com/tech-writing) | 🇬🇧 | Course |
| [dev.to / Hashnode / Medium](https://dev.to/) | 🇬🇧 | Platform |

**Deliverable:** published technical article about one of your artifacts (e.g., "How I evaluated RAG quality with groundedness"). Link it from LinkedIn and GitHub.

## Week 20 — Technical publication #2

**Objective:** consolidate your technical voice with a second topic (architecture or security).

**Deliverable:** second publication (e.g., "Threat modeling an AI system with OWASP LLM and MITRE ATLAS"). Goal: 2 pieces that show technical range + judgment.

## Week 21 — CP4 close: visibility + networking
