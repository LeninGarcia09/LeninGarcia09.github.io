# 🛡️ AI Security Professional

> **Professional Development Plan: From IT Manager to AI-Enabled Technology Leader**
>
> **9-month program** for professionals with experience in IT Management, digital forensics, and some programming who want to position themselves in the AI-era job market (2026-2030).

## 🎯 Candidate Profile

- Experience in **IT management** and operations
- Background in **digital forensics** and cybersecurity
- Basic **programming** knowledge
- Primary language: **Spanish** (English resources when critical)
- Need for immediate **career repositioning**

## 🏆 Program Objective

Transform the professional profile from:

> *"IT Manager with technical experience"*

To:

> *"AI-enabled Technology Leader with experience in cybersecurity, cloud, automation, governance, data analytics, and AI implementation, backed by certifications and a portfolio of real projects."*

## 🎓 Certifications — Vendor-Neutral Ladder (free first)

:::info[This plan is NOT "Microsoft first"]
The goal is to **return to AI employment as soon as possible**, in any industry and on any platform. That is why credentials are ordered by **employability value and cost**, not by vendor. Start with the **free and vendor-neutral** options (NIST, OWASP, ISO); add **one** vendor certification only when you know which stack your target employer uses (Microsoft, Google, or AWS), as a complement, not as the foundation.
:::

**Level 0 — Free and vendor-neutral foundations (start here)**

| Resource | Issuer | Cost | Why |
|---------|--------|-------|---------|
| [NIST AI RMF 1.0](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) + [GenAI Profile (AI 600-1)](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1) | NIST | Free | Common AI risk management language in any JD |
| [OWASP Top 10 for LLMs](https://genai.owasp.org/llm-top-10/) | OWASP | Free | Required knowledge in AI security roles |
| [MITRE ATLAS](https://atlas.mitre.org/) | MITRE | Free | Natural bridge from your cybersecurity background |
| [Elements of AI](https://www.elementsofai.com/) | U. Helsinki | Free (free cert) | Conceptual foundation, in Spanish |
| [IBM SkillsBuild — AI + Responsible AI](https://skillsbuild.org/) | IBM | Free (badges) | Stackable verifiable credentials |

**Level 1 — Vendor-neutral certifications (the real differentiator)**

| Cert | Issuer | Approx. cost (USD) | Prerequisite | Note |
|------|--------|--------------------|---------------|------|
| **AIGP** — AI Governance Professional | IAPP | ~$649–799 | None | De facto credential in AI governance |
| **PMI-CPMAI** — Managing AI | PMI | ~$699 + membership | None | **Exam in Spanish (LATAM)**; high value for PM/TPM |
| **CEET** — Certified Ethical Emerging Technologist | CertNexus | ~$270–350 | None | Lower-cost option in ethics/AI |
| **Lead Auditor ISO/IEC 42001** | Various | Free toolkit → $400+ | None | AI management system; momentum from the EU AI Act |

**Level 2 — Vendor-neutral security and audit**

| Cert | Issuer | Approx. cost (USD) | Prerequisite |
|------|--------|--------------------|---------------|
| **CC** — Certified in Cybersecurity | ISC2 | Free exam (1M program) | None |
| **CompTIA Security+** | CompTIA | ~$404 | None |
| **AAIA** — Advanced in AI Audit | ISACA | ~$459–599 | ⚠️ **Requires CISA** (or another audit designation) |

**Level 3 — Vendor options (choose your target employer's stack, as a complement)**

| Vendor | Entry credential | Cost | Exam language |
|-----------|-----------------------|-------|---------------|
| **Microsoft** | AZ-900, SC-900, AI-900, GH-900 → *(advanced)* [**AI-103** Azure AI Apps and Agents Developer](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/azure-ai-apps-and-agents-developer-associate/) | ~$99 each | Spanish available |
| **Google** | [Google AI Essentials](https://grow.google/ai-essentials/) / Cloud Digital Leader | Low cost / free audit | Spanish available |
| **AWS** | AWS Certified AI Practitioner | ~$100 | Spanish available |

:::note[AI-102 retired — use AI-103]
Microsoft **retired AI-102 on Jun 30, 2026**; its successor is **AI-103 (Azure AI Apps and Agents Developer Associate)**. For **governance / program / TPM** roles, AI-103 is *optional and advanced* (developer-oriented): it is usually better to prioritize AIGP/CPMAI + portfolio + frameworks (NIST/ISO). See [Sources and Verification](./sources-and-verification).
:::

:::tip[For someone between jobs: free first]
The full **Level 0 is free** and already makes you employable in AI governance and security conversations. Invest in a paid exam (Level 1) only once you have a portfolio and a clear target role. Many vendor certifications have **free vouchers** (Microsoft *Virtual Training Days*) and **Coursera Financial Aid** can unlock paid courses at no cost. Always verify price and syllabus on the official page before scheduling. Details in [Sources and Verification](./sources-and-verification).
:::
