# Reality check: skills coverage is not senior-job readiness

**Verdict:** A and B are foundation paths, not sufficient preparation for these senior vacancies. C is a better technical starting point, but still lacks role-specific application/agent depth and cannot establish professional AI experience. Add the [Senior Delivery Bridge](./senior-delivery-bridge) for experienced engineers rather than compressing years of delivery into another certificate.

**Audited on October 13, 2026.** This evaluates the written A/B/C curricula, not any person's proficiency. No learner code, employment history, interview performance, or English assessment was supplied. **Individual readiness is unknown.** Four postings from one employer are a useful target sample, not a representative market survey.

## Changes made from this audit

A now specifies Python, uv/Ruff, FastAPI/Pydantic/Uvicorn, PostgreSQL, persistent vector retrieval, a first framework/MCP exercise, and technical English practice **within its foundation scope**. B inherits these changes. Its hours remain a planning estimate; extend phases when the extra work requires it.

C retains its stronger ML/statistics/data purpose and adds explicit tooling/experiment evidence plus a route to the bridge. It is not rewritten as an agent-only plan.

The new **24-week Senior Delivery Bridge** is a proposed 360-hour upskilling program: 16 shared weeks plus one 8-week branch. It is **not** a new entry-level path or a replacement for experience. Learners may skip only a module whose evidence they can reproduce; not merely a tool name they recognize.

For these targets, shift attention from paid exam preparation to deployment, troubleshooting, framework/data integrations, and communication. AWS is the practical default for the E2 branch; E1 explicitly accepts AWS or GCP. E3/E4 do not justify a single mandatory vendor. AI-103 remains optional for Azure jobs, not the default credential for these four.

## Experience gate and honest next action

Create a private evidence ledger with dates, role, professional/project context, exact contribution, production vs sandbox status, users, releases, incidents, and what can be shared legally. Do not expose customer materials or inflate overlapping months.

| Starting situation | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| New programmer/career switcher | B/A, then suitable junior/backend/AI integration work; gather real delivery experience |
| Experienced software engineer without production AI | C or gap-based foundations + bridge; seek supervised AI ownership in current work or an appropriate role |
| Experienced AI/ML engineer | Audit against the chosen posting; bridge only the failed technical gates and prepare verifiable delivery stories |
| Experience requirement not met | Be explicit; ask the recruiter whether adjacent experience is acceptable or target a different level |

There is **no fixed calendar promise** for acquiring eligible professional experience. A public demo, open-source contribution, or volunteer project can strengthen evidence but must not be relabeled as paid professional work or a year of production agent delivery.

Research supporting the enhancements: [Senior engineering resources](./senior-resources). Recheck vacancy availability and geography before applying; embedded expiry dates do not guarantee the role remains open.
