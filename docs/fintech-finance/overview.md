# Finance and FinTech: learn, analyze, explain

Build practical finance analysis skills through one connected portfolio case. The core path develops accounting fluency, spreadsheet and SQL analysis, financial modeling, data visualization, data-quality checks, and clear business communication. Continue into payment analysis, corporate finance, and investment analysis as your time and interests allow.

These are learning plans, not employment guarantees. A project is evidence of study—not professional experience, an employer deliverable, investment advice, or proof of eligibility for a vacancy.

## Choose your horizon

The paths are cumulative. Start with the 12-week core; continue only as far as your goals and capacity make useful.

| Path | Planning assumption | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [3 months](./path-3-months) | 12 weeks × 10 hours = **120 hours** | Reconciled finance analysis, a forecast, and a first portfolio project; begin role-matched applications when basic evidence is ready. |
| [6 months](./path-6-months) | 24 weeks × 10 hours = **240 hours** | Add a reproducible payments-analysis project, decision-ready reporting, and corporate-finance practice. |
| [9 months](./path-9-months) | 36 weeks × 10 hours = **360 hours** | Extend into valuation, investment reasoning, or a deeper corporate-finance case; prepare stronger interview evidence. |

Hours and duration are planning estimates, not provider promises. At five hours per week, allow roughly twice the calendar time. If a completion check is not met, use the remediation instruction before moving on.

## Direction

For a finance graduate or early-career learner with some spreadsheet and data-tool exposure, a practical sequence is:

1. **Core finance analysis:** statements, reconciliation, forecasting, variance analysis, SQL, and data visualization.
2. **Primary specialization — FinTech analysis:** payment-funnel measures and transaction economics using synthetic detail plus appropriately interpreted public aggregate data.
3. **Second layer — corporate finance:** working capital, budgeting, cash flow, and capital allocation.
4. **Optional extension — investment analysis:** company research, valuation drivers, scenarios, and a concise investment thesis with explicit limitations.

These areas overlap but are not interchangeable. **FinTech/payment operations** concerns transactions, customer/payment flows, controls, and financial products. **Cloud FinOps** means managing and optimizing cloud technology costs; it is a separate specialization and is not included as a synonym for FinTech.

## Candidate assumptions and privacy

The plan uses a **10-hour-per-week** budget and favors free learning resources and synthetic data. Those are planning assumptions because weekly availability, training budget, software access, work authorization, and the candidate’s detailed baseline have not been confirmed. Revisit them before choosing a certification or claiming a language level.

The public site intentionally does not publish the candidate’s personal education, language-test scores, employment history, or work authorization.

## Start with a practical baseline

Before week 1, attempt these short tasks without copying a finished solution. Save your work privately; do not upload personal or employer data.

| Skill | 30–60 minute exercise | Evidence to save |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Excel | Reconcile a small synthetic transaction list to a monthly summary; identify duplicates and missing values. | Formula-driven workbook, exception list, and tied total. |
| SQL | Filter and aggregate a small dataset; join a second table without inflating totals. | Query file and a note showing how totals were checked. |
| Power BI or equivalent | Trace one displayed metric back to its source; state its numerator, denominator, period, and unit. | Screenshot or mock-up plus a short metric definition. |
| Finance | Explain how three example transactions affect profit, cash, or both. | Brief written explanation and assumptions. |
| Communication | Explain one finding in English in two minutes and write a short summary in the preferred working language. | Recording or notes; have a reviewer mark unclear claims. |

If SQL or financial-statement basics are new, extend the first month and use the introductory links in [resources and evidence](./resources-and-evidence). A course completion badge is not a substitute for the exercise.
