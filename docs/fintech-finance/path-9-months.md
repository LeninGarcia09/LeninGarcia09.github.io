# 9 months: extend into valuation and strategic finance

Complete the [3-month core](./path-3-months) and [6-month extension](./path-6-months) first. This path assumes **10 hours weekly**, about **360 estimated total hours**. Weeks 25–36 add company analysis, valuation, capital allocation, and a final presentation. Investment analysis is an optional learning branch—not individualized investment advice.

Each week is planned for **10 hours**: approximately 6 hours learning, 3 applied work, and 1 communication/review. Continue weekly English explanation and data-quality/reconciliation checks; every two weeks, replan using finished work and application or interview feedback.

**Dependencies and remediation:** complete the weeks in order; each deliverable builds on the previous one and the 3- and 6-month plans. If a completion check is missed, pause, revisit the linked resource, reduce the work to one small example, verify one calculation by hand, and request a review. Repeat the check before moving on.

| 25 | Read financial statements and business context. Use the “Getting data” and “From financial disclosures to value” materials in NYU Stern’s [Valuation Online Class](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/webcastvalonline.htm) and a public issuer’s published reports. | Write a source register and a short description of the business model and reporting period. | Inputs link back to published materials; distinguish reported values from estimates. |
| 26 | Examine historical trends and operating drivers using the [NYU Stern valuation course and supporting tests](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/webcastvalonline.htm). | Calculate revenue, margin, working-capital, and cash-flow trends from sourced figures. | Recalculate key ratios independently and record definitions. |
| 27 | Build a forecast from operating assumptions. Review selected [NYU Stern corporate-finance sessions](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/webcastcfonline.htm). | Create a simple forecast tied to business drivers and financial statements. | Statements are internally consistent; show forecast periods and assumptions. |
| 28 | Understand cash-flow valuation. Use selected sessions and post-class tests from NYU Stern’s [Valuation Online Class](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/webcastvalonline.htm). | Create a basic discounted-cash-flow model with sourced or explicitly hypothetical assumptions. | Formulas trace to inputs; cash-flow and discount periods align. |
| 29 | Evaluate discount-rate and risk assumptions with selected [NYU Stern valuation sessions](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/webcastvalonline.htm). | Explain how changed assumptions affect value; distinguish market data from judgment. | Sensitivity outputs reproduce; no single discount-rate value is represented as certain. |
| 30 | Compare valuation methods and peer selection using NYU Stern’s [valuation course and application tools](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/webcastvalonline.htm). | Identify comparables with a written inclusion/exclusion rationale. | Units and periods are normalized; explain why no company is a perfect comparable. |
| 31 | Run sensitivity and scenario analysis. Use selected [NYU Stern valuation sessions/tests](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/webcastvalonline.htm). | Show a small sensitivity table and narrative on the largest value drivers. | Model responses are directionally sensible and inputs are visible; test formula boundaries. |
| 32 | Write an investment-analysis memo. Review the [NYU Stern valuation-course material](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/webcastvalonline.htm). | Summarize thesis, evidence, key assumptions, risks, and disconfirming information. | Claims cite sources; uncertainty and limitations are prominent. Refresh the role sample and application tracker. | Reviewer feedback is logged; next development step is chosen from evidence, not certificate accumulation. |

## What this plan does not prove

Study hours, simulated cases, free courses, and certificates do not establish years of professional finance/accounting/FinOps experience, authority over real client funds, regulatory competence, investment performance, or seniority. The long-term senior financial-analysis reference role includes a degree-field requirement and several years of direct experience; this curriculum cannot substitute for either. Verify each real posting’s eligibility requirements separately.

## After week 36

Maintain one learning artifact at a time, update examples from current public data, and keep a dated source log. Choose later training only when job-posting evidence or actual work reveals a specific gap. Review the [resource and credential notes](./resources-and-evidence) before spending on an exam.
