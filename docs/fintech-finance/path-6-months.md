# 6 months: deepen analysis and connect it to decisions

Complete the [3-month core](./path-3-months) first. Continue at the same assumed pace—**10 hours a week**, for about **240 hours total** by the end of month six. Weeks 13–24 strengthen payment analytics while introducing practical corporate-finance analysis. These are study estimates; extend any week whose acceptance check is not met.

Each week is planned for **10 hours**: approximately 6 hours learning, 3 applied work, and 1 communication/review. Continue the weekly English explanation and data-quality/reconciliation checks from the core; every two weeks, replan using finished work and application or interview feedback.

**Dependencies and remediation:** complete the weeks in order; each deliverable builds on the previous one and the 3-month core. If a completion check is missed, pause, revisit the linked resource, reduce the work to one small example, verify one calculation by hand, and request a review. Repeat the check before moving on.

## Choose a secondary branch

At month four, choose one focus for the next four weeks of extension:

- **Corporate finance / planning:** deepen budgeting, forecast drivers, reconciliations, working capital, and variance explanation.
- **Investment analysis:** add statement analysis and valuation foundations using [NYU Stern finance and valuation resources](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/home.htm). Do not describe a classroom valuation as investment advice.
- **Payments / FinTech analysis:** deepen transaction-funnel definitions, cohort comparisons, controls, and decision communication.

Use the same evidence standard in every branch: traceable inputs, reproducible calculations, explicit assumptions, reconciliations, and a clear explanation. The [9-month extension](./path-9-months) offers further valuation and strategy practice.

## Month-six checkpoint

By month six, aim to show two reproducible learning projects, one based on a finance case and one on a chosen specialty. Begin or continue applications when eligibility fits; do not postpone them solely to collect certificates. Reassess every two weeks using actual project quality and interview feedback.
